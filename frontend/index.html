DETECTION ARCHITECTURE

The Four Vectors of Academic & Employment Fraud

Scam solicitations targeting students rely on predictable psychological pressure and structural anomalies. TrustLens AI breaks down every message against four deterministic detection categories.

FIN-01 • FIN-02 • FIN-03

Financial Demands & Advance Fees

Detects mandatory registration fees, laptop equipment deposits, background check fees, training onboarding charges, counterfeit check overpayments, and requests for irreversible payment via Zelle, wire transfer, Bitcoin, or retail gift cards.

Typical Solicitation Pattern: “Pay $150 refundable deposit before Monday via Zelle for your laptop dispatch.”
URG-01 • URG-02

Artificial Urgency & Coercion

Identifies manufactured expiration deadlines, immediate forfeiture threats, coercive demands to respond within 24 hours, and instructions to keep offers confidential or bypass university career counselors.

Typical Solicitation Pattern: “Immediate response required within 24 hours or the offer will be permanently revoked!”
SENS-01 • SENS-02

Credential & OTP Harvesting

Catches deceptive attempts to extract SMS/email One-Time Passwords (OTPs), multi-factor codes, student portal login passwords, bank account PINs, or premature Social Security Numbers under the guise of “identity verification”.

Typical Solicitation Pattern: “Provide the 6-digit verification code (OTP) sent to your mobile to release the scholarship.”
URL-01 • URL-02 • URL-03

Suspicious URLs & Channel Redirection

Unmasks disguised links, URL shorteners (bit.ly, tinyurl), raw IP addresses, high-risk TLDs (.xyz, .top), lookalike brand domains, free webmail addresses spoofing corporate HR (@gmail.com), and rapid shifting to Telegram or WhatsApp.

Typical Solicitation Pattern: “Contact our recruiter on Telegram @google_careers or visit http://192.168.1.55.”

INTEGRITY & ARCHITECTURE

Privacy by Design. Honest Limitations.

We believe cybersecurity tools protecting students must respect user privacy and be transparent about their operational scope.

Privacy Architecture

  • 100% In-Memory RAM Processing: Text submitted for analysis is processed strictly in volatile server memory. Nothing is written to a database or stored on disk.
  • Zero Data Retention: No account required, no tracking cookies, and no telemetry tracking your identity or submitted content.
  • Local Python Execution: The deterministic rule engine runs locally within the FastAPI application. Your messages are never sent to external AI providers or cloud feeds.
  • Session-Only History: Recent assessments are stored strictly in browser JavaScript memory for the current session and never persist across page reloads.

Honest Scope & Limitations

  • Heuristic Pattern Evaluation: TrustLens detects recognized tactical indicators of fraud. A low risk score indicates few heuristic red flags were found, not an absolute legal guarantee.
  • No Live Commercial Threat Feeds: The application does not claim to cross-reference live commercial threat-intelligence databases. It operates via explainable local rules.
  • Original Message Never Saved in History: When you revisit a recent analysis, only the verdict and score metadata are restored; the raw message text is never retained.
  • Human Verification Required: TrustLens is designed to assist student skepticism, not replace due diligence. Always verify offers directly through registered university portals.

How TrustLens AI Operates

Section A

How Analysis Works

TrustLens AI operates on a deterministic, explainable baseline engine written in Python. When you submit an internship offer, scholarship message, or URL, the backend processes the text locally through multi-stage structural pattern matching, context window parsing, and negation analysis.

Unlike generative AI models that can hallucinate plausibility or guess without accountability, every finding in TrustLens maps directly to verifiable deception mechanisms documented in student recruitment fraud investigations.

Section B

Risk Score Interpretation (0–100)

The calculated risk score is a bounded heuristic index from 0 (very low risk) to 100 (critical risk). It aggregates weighted rule triggers, applies non-linear diminishing returns to prevent runaway inflation, discounts overlapping signals, and factors in observed hygiene indicators.

0–19: Low
20–44: Moderate
45–69: High
70–100: Critical
0–19 (Low): No recognized scam indicators detected; affirmative professional hygiene patterns present. Standard due diligence still advised.
20–44 (Moderate): Atypical solicitations, exaggerated pay promises, or informal communication shifts. Verification required.
45–69 (High): Substantial red flags (e.g. shortened links, guaranteed hiring without interview, brand spoofing via free webmail). Do not provide sensitive data.
70–100 (Critical): Acute threat indicators including mandatory upfront fees, OTP/password demands, fake check overpayments, or untraceable crypto/gift-card payment methods.

* Important: These bands are heuristic severity thresholds, not calibrated statistical probabilities of fraud or absolute legal guarantees.

Section C

Implemented Detection Signals

The engine actively inspects messages against six specialized detector categories:

FIN-01 / 02 / 03

Upfront Fees & Deposit Demands

Detects mandatory registration fees, laptop deposits, onboarding training charges, irreversible payment via retail gift cards/crypto/Zelle, and counterfeit check overpayment schemes.

URG-01 / 02

Artificial Urgency & Secrecy

Flags coercive deadlines (“within 2 hours”), immediate revocation threats, demands to keep offers confidential, and instructions to bypass formal HR or university channels.

SENS-01 / 02

OTP & Sensitive Credential Harvesting

Identifies attempts to extract SMS/email One-Time Passwords (OTPs), 2FA codes, account passwords, bank account PINs, or premature government identity numbers (SSN).

URL-01 / 02 / 03 / COMM

Suspicious URLs & Channel Redirection

Detects link shorteners (bit.ly, tinyurl), raw IP addresses, high-risk TLDs (.xyz, .top, .click), lookalike brand domains, free mailboxes (@gmail.com) claiming corporate identity, and rapid redirection to Telegram or WhatsApp.

EMP-01 / 02 / SCHOL-01

Employment & Scholarship Red Flags

Catches guaranteed selection without any evaluation, disproportionate pay for trivial tasks ($500/day typing), and pay-to-claim bogus scholarship awards.

CONTEXT-AWARE

Contextual Negation Safeguards

Distinguishes safe educational warnings (e.g. “never share your OTP”, “we do not charge fees”, cybersecurity lectures) to prevent false-positive alerts on benign safety advice.

Section D

Privacy Architecture & Limitations

  • Local Backend Processing: In the current deployment, your text is analyzed in volatile memory on the local server. Content is not stored in a database or used for model training.
  • No External API Dependency: The baseline detection engine runs entirely locally without transmitting your text to external LLMs or third-party threat feeds.
  • Session-Only Recent History: Recent analyses are stored strictly in JavaScript browser memory. Closing the tab or refreshing the page clears the history immediately.
  • Original Text Never Stored in History: The session history retains only high-level metadata (score, category, timestamp, findings summary); the original submitted text is never saved.
  • No Absolute Guarantee: A low score indicates few recognized heuristic red flags were detected—it does not constitute affirmative verification of the sender's identity.
  • Deployment Caveat: Local privacy applies to this local deployment; we do not claim full privacy under every possible multi-tenant or unencrypted hosting architecture.
